How it works

Three steps, no setup.

Run your test

Export results from Postman (JSON) or save your JMeter run as CSV / JTL.

Choose and upload

Pick the report type above, then drag and drop your file. Nothing is sent to a server.

Read, decide, share

Get a verdict, charts and details. Export as PDF, HTML or JSON for your team or pipeline.

Built for clear decisions

Less noise, more answers.

Real pass / fail

Status is calculated from your actual results against thresholds you control.

Adjustable criteria

Set your own error-rate and response-time limits, or use the defaults for each test type.

Charts that matter

Response time, errors, throughput and users over time, without the clutter.

Share anywhere

Export to PDF or HTML, or a JSON summary you can use in CI/CD.

Works on any screen

Responsive layout with light and dark themes.

Made to grow

A simple registry means new report types slot in without rewrites.

Your data stays with you

Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your test files are never uploaded or stored.
