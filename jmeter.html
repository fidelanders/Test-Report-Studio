Load, stress & performance test summary

Test settings

Verdicts use error rate, p95 and p99; throughput is optional. Load/soak use explicit acceptance thresholds. Stress reports the breaking point (first interval with over 5% errors or p95 past the limit) and also grades the error rate of the whole run (defaults: pass ≤ 20%, warn ≤ 40%). Spike is scored on the surge itself (users at 50% of peak or more) and also checks recovery. Defaults follow the selected test type; edit a value to override.
